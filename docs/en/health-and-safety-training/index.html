Health and safety (BHP) training

We run the mandatory Polish BHP training for your staff, including people from abroad. We come to your company and fit around your shift schedule.

from 99 PLN per person, group prices on request
Sign up for this course How often training is required

Training we run

  • Induction training: general instruction for new employees.
  • Refresher training for manual, office and engineering-technical roles.
  • Refresher training for employers and managers.

Who it's for

  • For construction, manufacturing, warehouse and logistics companies.
  • For employers with staff from abroad: training in English or Ukrainian.
  • Group prices on request.

How often training is required

We run the course on our yard or at your company, in English, Polish or Ukrainian. We give you the schedule and dates when you sign up.

  • First refresher training: within 12 months of starting for manual, office and technical roles, and within 6 months for managers.
  • Manual workers: at least every 3 years.
  • Employers, managers and engineering-technical staff: at least every 5 years.
  • Office staff: at least every 6 years.

Questions about this course

How much is BHP training?

From 99 PLN gross per person. We prepare individual quotes for groups.

Do you come to companies?

Yes. We train at your premises anywhere in Poland, most often in the south.

Does each employee get a certificate?

Yes. Every participant receives a training completion certificate.

Where we train

We train across Poland, most often in Silesia, Lesser Poland and Subcarpathia, including Katowice, Gliwice, Bielsko-Biała, Częstochowa, Kraków, Tarnów, Nowy Sącz and Rzeszów. Our instructor travels to companies.
