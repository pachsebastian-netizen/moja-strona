How the course works

One instructor takes you from sign-up to certificate, so you always know what comes next.

  1. 1

    Sign up

    Fill in the form or write to us. We agree the date, place and language.

  2. 2

    Theory

    How the machine is built and operated, technical inspection rules and safety. We go through the UDT exam questions.

  3. 3

    Practice

    Driving and manoeuvres on the yard until you feel confident at the controls.

  4. 4

    UDT exam

    We register you for the exam before the UDT board and take care of the formalities.

  5. 5

    Certification

    Pass the exam and you receive a UDT qualification certificate, valid across Poland.

Frequently asked questions

Does the course price include the UDT exam?

No. The exam fee is charged by the Office of Technical Inspection (UDT). In 2026 it is 333,88 PLN for each certification category. We help you pay it and add nothing on top.

Who issues the certification?

The Office of Technical Inspection (UDT). After passing the exam you receive a qualification certificate that is valid across Poland.

Does the telehandler course also cover forklifts?

Yes. Certification for variable-reach trucks, that is telehandlers, also covers standard forklifts.

Can I train in English or Ukrainian?

Yes. We teach in Polish, English and Ukrainian. For the UDT exam we can arrange an interpreter we work with.

Do you come to companies?

Yes, anywhere in Poland, most often in the south. We train at your site on your equipment.

How long does a course take?

It depends on the course and the group size. We give you the exact schedule when you sign up.
